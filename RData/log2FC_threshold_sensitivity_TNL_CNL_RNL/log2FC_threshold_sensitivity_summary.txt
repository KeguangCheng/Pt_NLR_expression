8. Key overall interpretation
Under LRT-only criterion, the number of time-responsive TNL/CNL/RNL NLRs is 181.
Under strict |max log2FC| >= 1 filtering, only 179 of 181 LRT-DE NLRs would be retained, while 2 would be discarded. Lost ratio = 0.011.

Recommended reporting strategy:
Use LRT adjusted p-value < 0.05 as the primary definition of time-responsive NLRs, and report |max log2FC| >= 1, 0.75, 0.5, and 0.25 as threshold sensitivity analyses.
