7. Suggested interpretation
Modules should be prioritized by enrichment statistics, not raw NLR count alone. A large module can contain many NLRs simply because it contains many genes, whereas a smaller module with high DE TCR NLR density and high fold enrichment is more likely to represent an NLR-centered response module.
